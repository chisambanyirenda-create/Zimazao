ESCROW PAYMENT SYSTEM

	•	When a buyer pays online the money is held by Flutterwave and not released to the farmer immediately

	•	Only after confirmation does the money get released to the farmer minus 3 percent commission
	•	If buyer does not confirm after 48 hours the system automatically releases the money to the farmer
	•	If there is a dispute buyer clicks Report Problem button and the transaction is frozen until the CEO resolves it from the admin panel

CASH ON DELIVERY OPTION

	•	Buyer can choose Pay Online or Cash on Delivery when placing an order
	•	For Cash on Delivery no money is collected online
	•	The system records the order and both farmer and buyer get notified
	•	When they meet and complete the deal the farmer marks the order as Completed
	•	Zimazao sends the farmer an invoice for the 3 percent commission to be paid manually or deducted from their next online transaction

ORDER TRACKING SYSTEM

	•	Every order has a status that updates in real time
	•	Statuses are: Order Placed, Confirmed by Farmer, Ready for Pickup or Delivery, Dispatched, Delivered, Completed, Disputed, Refunded
	•	Both buyer and farmer see the same status updating live
	•	Each status change sends a notification to both parties
	•	Every status change is recorded with timestamp so there is a full audit trail

DISPUTE RESOLUTION SYSTEM

	•	Buyer or farmer can raise a dispute on any order
	•	They must select a reason: Wrong product, Wrong quantity, Product damaged, Payment issue, Other
	•	They can upload photos as evidence
	•	The dispute appears immediately in the CEO admin panel as urgent
	•	CEO can resolve by approving refund, releasing payment to farmer, or requesting more information
	•	Both parties are notified of the resolution

TRANSACTION RECORDS

	•	Every single transaction is recorded in the database with full details
	•	Records include: order id, buyer name, farmer name, crop, quantity, amount paid, commission taken, farmer payout, payment method, status, every status change with timestamp
	•	Nobody can delete transaction records not even the CEO, they can only be marked
	•	CEO can export all transactions as CSV or PDF from admin panel

REFUND SYSTEM

	•	CEO can issue full or partial refund from admin panel
	•	Refund goes back to the original payment method
	•	Farmer is notified of refund and reason
	•	Refund is recorded in transaction history

FARMER WALLET

	•	Each farmer has a wallet inside Zimazao showing their earnings
	•	Wallet shows: total earned, pending clearance, available for withdrawal, commission deducted
	•	Farmer can request withdrawal from wallet to their mobile money
	•	Withdrawal requests appear in CEO admin panel for approval
	•	CEO approves and processes through Flutterwave

TRUST AND SAFETY FEATURES

	•	After every completed order both parties rate each other
	•	Listings from highly rated farmers appear higher in search

	•	Farmers with repeated disputes get automatically suspended
